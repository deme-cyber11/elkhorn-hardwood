Our most requested service transforms tired, scratched, and faded hardwood floors back to their original beauty — or better. Whether you own a 1960s ranch in Chalco with original red oak or a 2010s Millard home with builder-grade flooring, whole-house refinishing is the most cost-effective way to dramatically improve your home's look and value.

Our Refinishing Process

Every project begins with a thorough floor assessment — board thickness, moisture levels, problem areas, and existing finish condition. Here's what happens next:

  1. Preparation — Mask baseboards, vents, and transitions. Remove doors. Set nails below surface.
  2. Coarse Sanding — 36-grit removes old finish and levels the floor. Decades of wear disappear.
  3. Medium & Fine Sanding — 60-grit and 100-grit passes create a smooth, uniform surface.
  4. Staining (Optional) — Choose from 40+ colors. Test patches applied in your home's lighting for approval.
  5. Finish Application — 2–3 coats of Bona Mega ONE waterborne poly, rated for Nebraska's UV and low humidity.
  6. Final Inspection — Walk-through together. Written care guide and 30-day follow-up check included.
Seasonal timing: Finishes cure faster in dry winter air than on humid summer days. We plan coat timing around the weather so every coat cures properly.

A 1,200 sq ft home averages $4,200 to $8,400. See our complete pricing guide for detailed breakdowns by project size and finish type.

Dustless Hardwood Floor Sanding

Traditional sanding fills your home with fine wood dust — cabinets, HVAC ducts, electronics, everything. Fine particles can hang in the air for hours, and a running furnace or air conditioner pulls them into the ducts. Dustless sanding eliminates this entirely.

How It Works

Commercial-grade HEPA-filtered vacuums connect directly to every sanding tool, capturing 99% of dust at the source. HEPA air scrubbers run continuously to filter the remaining ambient air down to 0.3-micron particles.

Why It Matters in Your Home

Stay in Your Home

No plastic sheeting. Many families stay during the project.

Protect Your HVAC

Furnaces run hard in Nebraska winters — dust in ducts recirculates for months.

Better Finish

Less airborne dust means fewer particles in wet finish coats.

Pet & Allergy Safe

Important for anyone with allergies or asthma.

Pricing: Dustless sanding is included in every refinish at no extra charge, inside our $3.50 to $7/sq ft refinishing range. Many contractors charge extra for it, often around $2 per sq ft.

Nothing transforms a home faster than changing floor color. That orangey 1990s golden oak? We can take it to rich espresso, modern cool gray, classic natural, or any of 40+ colors. Elkhorn homeowners are choosing darker tones and weathered grays that suit both modern and farmhouse interiors.

Our Color Consultation

We don't show swatches on a card. We bring our full stain library to your home and apply 3–5 test patches directly on your floor, in your lighting. Stain looks completely different on red oak vs. white oak vs. maple — and different under south-facing windows vs. overhead LEDs. You approve the exact color before we start.

Building in Bennington or Waterloo? Replacing carpet with hardwood? We install solid and engineered hardwood with the same precision we bring to refinishing. With Nebraska's humidity swings, acclimation and moisture management are critical.

Installation Methods

Nail-Down

Traditional method for 3/4" solid hardwood over plywood or OSB subfloors.

Glue-Down

For engineered hardwood over concrete. Moisture-barrier adhesive prevents vapor issues.

Floating

Click-lock engineered planks. Fastest method, budget-friendly, forgiving on varied subfloors.

Acclimation matters: Wood often arrives from the mill or warehouse with a different moisture content than your home. We require 7–10 days of acclimation in your home and verify moisture within 2% of subfloor before installation day.

Species We Install

Red oak, white oak, hickory, maple, walnut, Brazilian cherry, and engineered options. Hickory is increasingly popular here for its hardness and rustic grain that suits farmhouse and craftsman-style homes. We help you choose based on lifestyle, traffic, and aesthetics.
