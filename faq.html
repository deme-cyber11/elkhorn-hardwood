Hardwood Floor Refinishing FAQ

Answers to the most common questions Elkhorn homeowners ask about hardwood floor refinishing, costs, timelines, and our process.

Hardwood floor refinishing in Elkhorn typically costs $3.50 to $7 per square foot, depending on the condition of your floors and the finish you choose. A standard 1,000 sq ft refinishing job runs $3,500 to $7,000 including sanding, staining, and three coats of polyurethane. Dustless sanding is included at no extra charge. We provide free on-site estimates with exact pricing and no hidden fees. See our complete pricing guide for detailed breakdowns by project size and finish type.

A typical whole-house refinishing project takes 3–5 days for a 1,000–1,500 sq ft home. Day one is sanding, day two is staining (if you're changing colors), and days three through five are finish coats with drying time between. You can walk on the floors in socks after 24 hours and move furniture back after 72 hours. Cure times change with the seasons. Finishes dry faster in dry winter air than on humid summer days, so we plan coat timing around the weather.

Dustless sanding uses industrial HEPA-filtered vacuum systems attached directly to the sanding equipment, capturing 99% of wood dust at the source. Traditional sanding fills your home with fine dust that settles on every surface and infiltrates HVAC ducts. Fine sanding dust can stay airborne for hours, which makes dustless sanding especially important for families with allergies or asthma. Our dustless system means no plastic sheeting, no post-project deep cleaning, and safe air quality throughout the process. Learn more about our dustless sanding process.

Refinishing costs 60–75% less than replacement and takes half the time. If your hardwood is structurally sound — no severe warping, rot, or subfloor damage — refinishing is almost always the better investment. Solid hardwood floors can be refinished 4–6 times over their lifespan. Replacement only makes sense when boards are thinner than 3/4 inch from previous sandings or when you want an entirely different wood species. We assess your floor thickness during our free estimate and give you an honest recommendation. Compare refinishing vs. replacement costs.

Absolutely. Elkhorn sits at about 1,150 feet in eastern Nebraska, with humid summers and cold winters. Furnace-dried winter air causes hardwood to contract, creating gaps between boards. That's normal seasonal movement, not a defect. Strong sun through south- and west-facing windows also fades unfinished or poorly finished wood over time. We use UV-resistant finishes and recommend running a humidifier to keep indoor humidity at 35–45% year-round. These Nebraska-specific considerations are built into every project we plan.

Every refinishing project includes our 3-year finish warranty covering adhesion failure, peeling, bubbling, and premature wear under normal use. We use Bona Mega ONE commercial-grade finish and NWFA-certified application techniques, so warranty claims are extremely rare. The warranty doesn't cover damage from pet accidents, dragged furniture without felt pads, or standing water — but those are preventable with the basic care guide we provide after every job.

Yes — custom stain matching is one of our specialties. We carry 40+ stain samples and can blend custom colors on-site to match existing trim, stairs, or adjacent rooms. For additions or repairs where you need a seamless blend, we apply test patches directly on your floor so you can approve the color in your home's lighting before we commit to the full area. Learn more about our stain color options.

Move all furniture off the hardwood areas before our crew arrives — we can recommend local Elkhorn movers if you need help. Remove wall hangings and curtains near floor level to prevent dust settling on them. Clear closet floors if those are being refinished too. We handle all baseboards, transitions, and floor vents as part of our standard process. Plan for 3–5 days where you'll need to avoid the work areas. Contact us for a detailed preparation checklist.

With our dustless sanding system, most families stay in their homes during the project. You'll need to avoid the active work areas, but you can use other rooms normally. We use low-VOC Bona waterborne finishes that produce minimal odor — far less than traditional oil-based polyurethane. If you have severe chemical sensitivities, we recommend staying elsewhere during finish application days (typically 1–2 days). Many Elkhorn homeowners with kids and pets stay home throughout the entire process.

It depends on the wear layer thickness. Engineered hardwood with a wear layer of 2mm or more can typically be sanded and refinished once. Floors with thicker 4mm+ wear layers can be refinished multiple times — similar to solid hardwood. We measure the wear layer during our free estimate to determine if your engineered floor is a candidate for refinishing. Many Elkhorn homes built in the 2000s–2010s have engineered hardwood that's ready for its first refinish.

We exclusively use Bona commercial-grade waterborne polyurethane finishes. Our standard is Bona Mega ONE — a single-component finish that's 3x more durable than standard poly and rated for Nebraska's intense UV. For high-traffic areas like hallways, kitchens, and commercial spaces, we offer Bona Traffic HD, a two-component finish with even greater durability and scratch resistance. Both are low-VOC, low-odor, and non-yellowing — they won't turn amber over time like old oil-based finishes.

Most hardwood floors in Elkhorn need refinishing every 7–10 years with normal residential use. High-traffic areas like hallways and kitchens may need attention sooner — every 5–7 years. Homes with dogs typically need refinishing on the shorter end due to claw scratches. Proper maintenance extends the life of your finish: use felt pads on all furniture legs, place area rugs in high-traffic zones, keep indoor humidity at 35–45%, and clean with Bona-approved hardwood floor cleaner (no vinegar or Swiffer WetJet).
