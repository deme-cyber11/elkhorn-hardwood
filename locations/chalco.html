Scratched, Dull, and Stained — But Not Beyond Saving

Hardwood floors that look like they need to be replaced usually don't. Refinishing removes years of wear by sanding to bare wood and building a fresh finish from scratch — what comes back looks like new installation and costs a fraction of replacement. Chalco homes see significant humidity swings between summer and winter, which stresses finishes faster than in moderate climates. Most of what looks like floor damage is actually finish failure — and a full refinish fixes it completely.

What Waiting Is Doing to Your Floors

  • Scratches go deeper over time. Once the finish layer is broken, bare wood absorbs grit and moisture. The damage that stops at the surface today doesn't stay there.
  • Water damage is cumulative. Repeated moisture events penetrate further into the wood each season. There's a point where refinishing stops being possible.
  • Pre-sale window is short. Floor refinishing requires cure time before furniture goes back. If you're listing, you need this done before the rush.
  • Replacement costs about twice as much. Refinishing at $3.50–$7/sqft vs. new hardwood at $6–$12/sqft. If the wood underneath is good, refinishing is almost always the right call.

How We Refinish Floors in Chalco

1Free in-home estimate. We assess species, thickness, condition, and give you a written quote — full refinish, screen-and-recoat, or board replacement.

2Stain samples in your home. You pick the color on your actual floor, in your actual light. You approve before we commit.

3Dustless sanding. A containment-and-vacuum system captures dust at the source — dramatically cleaner than traditional refinishing.

4Finish coats. Two to three coats of commercial-grade polyurethane, buffed between each. Water-based options for faster return and lower odor.

5Cure and handoff. Light foot traffic in 24 hours (water-based) or 48 hours (oil-based). Written protocol before we leave.

Quick Answers Before You Call

Will there be a lot of dust?

Not with our dustless sanding system. It captures the vast majority of sanding dust at the source — dramatically different from traditional refinishing.

What if I choose the wrong stain color?

You choose from test patches on your actual floor, in your actual light. You approve it before we proceed.

How long until I can use the room?

Water-based finish: foot traffic in 24 hours, furniture in 72. Oil-based: 48 hours for foot traffic, 7 days for furniture.
